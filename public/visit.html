The room

What used to be dark is open and white and bright

The space was a wine bar called Cave a Vin. Maria and Lorena bought it from the old owner, spent five months on it, and turned a darker room with its burgundy ceiling into something else entirely — “open and white and bright and really nice.” At the soft opening the old owner stopped by, saw the new room, and cried.

The team

From the owners of Blue Tortuga

Uncorked comes from Lorena San Martin and Maria Huertas, who opened Blue Tortuga Bistro & Bar on Chapel Street in early 2025. “Blue Tortuga is more like party time, like casual,” San Martin says. “We wanted to do something completely different.” This is the other end of New Haven, and a different kind of crowd.

Common questions

Before you come

When did Uncorked Wine Bar open?

The bar opened with a soft opening in November 2026 and held its grand opening on Wednesday, November 25, 2026, at 975 State Street in New Haven.

Where are you located?

975 State Street, New Haven, Connecticut 06511 - on Upper State Street in the East Rock neighborhood.

What are your hours?

Four o'clock in the afternoon every day: Sunday through Thursday until 1 am, Friday and Saturday until 2 am.

How much is a glass of wine?

It depends on the bottle, but the Cabernet the room keeps ordering - Bonanza from Caymus Vineyards - is $14 a glass.

Do you serve food?

Yes - small plates, including filet mignon sliders with beet chips, steak tartare, octopus and hummus, and chicken liver with lingonberry.
