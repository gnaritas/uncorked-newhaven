Privacy

What this site collects

This site collects only what you send it on purpose. The contact form takes your name, your email, an optional phone number, and your note. That message goes to Uncorked Wine Bar’s inbox and is used for one thing: answering you.

No cookies are set by this site. No analytics or tracking scripts run here. Nothing you type is sold, shared, or added to some other company’s mailing list.

If you’d rather we didn’t keep your message after we’ve answered it, say so in the note and we’ll delete it.
