How the list happened

Five months, a lot of tastings

The room used to be a wine bar called Cave a Vin. Maria Huertas and Lorena San Martin bought it from the old owner, kept it a wine bar, and rebuilt the cellar from zero — the old stock out, a new list in, chosen over months of tastings.

“So we actually picked and took our time to make the wine list ourselves. We had a lot of tastings, a lot of drunken days.”

Lorena San Martin · co-owner
